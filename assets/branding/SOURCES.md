# Branding sources

PNG cards for the profile owner's professional accounts and institutions.

- CEIA: updated `logo-ceia.png` supplied by the user.
- INF / UFG: user-supplied `INF-compact-white.png`, from the Desktop `lib/logos` collection. Destination: https://inf.ufg.br/
- LinkedIn: Devicon v2.17.0, https://github.com/devicons/devicon/blob/v2.17.0/icons/linkedin/linkedin-original.svg
- ORCID: Simple Icons, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/orcid.svg ; rendered in the ORCID brand green.
- Lattes symbol: Academicons by James Walsh, https://github.com/jpswalsh/academicons/blob/master/svg/lattes.svg ; SIL Open Font License 1.1, https://github.com/jpswalsh/academicons/blob/master/LICENSE .
- Email: generic envelope drawn as SVG and rendered as PNG.

The supplied institutional logos retain their proportions and artwork. Contact labels are white on dark cards. Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners.
